About Maya

A family-run kitchen

Welcome to Maya Asian Restaurant, a small, family-run business serving authentic Nepali and Indian cuisine in Harrisburg, PA.

From flavorful momos and delicious curries to freshly prepared traditional favorites, we bring the taste of home to your table. Every dish is made with care, passion, and a love for sharing good food.

Thank you for supporting our family business!

Himalayan staples

Momo in four styles, thukpa noodle soup, and sekuwa grilled the traditional way.

Tandoor & curry house

Classic kadai, bhuna, korma and vindaloo, plus tandoori meats and fresh naan.

Vegetarian-friendly

Paneer, dal, aloo and vegetable versions run through nearly every section.
