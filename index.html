Why Maya

Maya, where every guest feels at home

Welcome to Maya Asian Restaurant, a small, family-run business serving authentic Nepali and Indian cuisine in Harrisburg, PA.

From flavorful momos and delicious curries to freshly prepared traditional favorites, we bring the taste of home to your table. Every dish is made with care, passion, and a love for sharing good food.

Thank you for supporting our family business!

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you have vegetarian options?+

Yes — look for the green dot next to a dish in our menu. Vegetable momo, paneer dishes, dal tadka and more run through nearly every section.

Can dishes be made more or less spicy?+

Spice levels vary by dish — let us know if you'd like something milder or spicier when you order.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?+

Yes. Order pickup directly through our online menu, or get it delivered through DoorDash or Grubhub.

Is Maya Asian Restaurant family-owned?+

Yes — Maya is a small, family-run restaurant serving Harrisburg. Every dish is made to order.
